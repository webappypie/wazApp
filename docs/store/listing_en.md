## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Is WhatsApp consuming all your phone's storage? 

**WappTidy** is the smart, privacy-first media organizer designed specifically for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business. Reclaim gigabytes of space in minutes with zero guesswork and zero risk to your cherished memories.

### 🌟 Why WappTidy is Different:
Most cleaner apps request dangerous full-disk permissions and secretly upload your data. WappTidy runs **100% on-device**, follows strict Scoped Storage standards, and **NEVER auto-deletes anything**. You are always in control.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

#### 1. 🔍 Exact Duplicate Detection
Find and remove identical photos, videos, and audio clips using a lightning-fast two-stage binary hash engine. Automatically pre-selects excess copies while keeping the original.

#### 2. 📸 Similar Photos & Best Shot
Took 5 shots to get the perfect picture? Our 64-bit perceptual hashing detects burst photos and similar angles, highlighting the best shot so you can clear the rest.

#### 3. 🌫️ Intelligent Blur Detection
Identifies blurry, out-of-focus, and low-quality photos using multi-scale Laplacian variance. Intelligently protects intentional portrait shots with shallow depth of field.

#### 4. 🎬 Smart Video Compressor
Compress bulky WhatsApp videos by up to 70% without visible quality loss. Compare original vs. compressed quality with side-by-side verification before deciding.

#### 5. 👆 Swipe Clean Mode
Clean up clutter with fun, intuitive gestures! Swipe right to keep, swipe left to trash, with an instant undo button and batch review before taking action.

#### 6. 🎙️ Voice Notes & Audio Cleaner
Listen before you clean! Built-in audio player with scrub slider and 1.5x / 2x speed playback ensures you never accidentally delete an important voice message.

#### 7. 📄 Documents & Archive Manager
Easily sort through shared PDFs, spreadsheets, Word documents, and zip files. Open and inspect documents with your favorite system viewer before clearing them out.

#### 8. 📈 Storage Growth Analytics
Understand your WhatsApp storage habits with interactive trend graphs. Cold-start backfill reconstructs your media history from day one.

#### 9. ☁️ Google Drive Backup
Offload media to the cloud before cleaning. Uses Google's strictly restricted `drive.file` scope—WappTidy can only access files it creates, guaranteeing your personal Google Drive remains completely private.

#### 10. 🛡️ Ad-Free 30-Day Recycle Bin
Accidentally trashed a photo? The local Recycle Bin holds files for 30 days. Enjoy a 100% ad-free experience in the Bin so you can restore items stress-free.

### 🔒 Privacy & Safety Pledges (P-01 to P-03)
- **Zero Cloud Upload of Media**: All indexing, hashing, blur checks, and duplicate detection happen entirely on your phone.
- **Never Auto-Delete**: Background scans detect opportunities only. We never delete files without your explicit confirmation.
- **No Broad Storage Permissions**: Built according to modern Android Scoped Storage guidelines (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` is never used).
- **Accurate Numbers Only**: No fake boost percentages or simulated savings—every megabyte shown traces directly to real files.
