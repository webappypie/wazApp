# Store Listing Draft — WazApp

**Working name:** WazApp  
**Tagline:** Save. Style. Share.

## Short description draft
Save accessible status media, edit photos and videos, create text statuses and share your creations.

## Full description draft
WazApp brings status saving, local media management and quick creative tools together in one convenient app.

**Save and organise** — Browse and save status media your device and granted permissions make accessible; organise photos and videos locally; preview, favourite, search, share and manage saved items.

**Create your style** — Make supported photo/video edits, add text and stickers, create text-based status images with Hindi and English, and export/share through supported device options.

**Privacy-minded** — Core media workflows are designed to work locally. No account is required for core features, and media is not silently uploaded to an app-owned media server.

Feature availability depends on device, OS, format and permissions. On iOS, use supported Photos/Files pickers; WazApp does not claim access to WhatsApp's private app storage. WazApp is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

Do not publish until claims match verified implementation and legal/store review is complete. Verify trademark/store-name/domain, privacy policy, data disclosures and final-build screenshots.
